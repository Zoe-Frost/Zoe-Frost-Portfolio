About me

Since I was little, I've been drawn to creating, whether it was painting, sculpting, or drawing, I loved finding different projects that I could express myself through.

Now, as a designer I am driven by curiosity and a passion to create thoughtful and engaging experiences that connect with others.

I enjoy transforming ideas into user focused solutions through iteration and feedback from real users which help to further improve my initial ideas and is an essential part of my process.

My values

Growth

I value growth due to the fact that I am always looking to learn, and evolve through new experiences and challenges in both my professional and personal life.

Empathy

Empathy is important to me because I enjoy the process of understanding and connecting with others and in turn creating stronger relationships with those people and creating their experience to be as meaningful as it can be.

Innovation

Innovation is very important to me as someone who has come from an artistic background. When exploring new technology, styles, and techniques, it inspires me and pushes me to go further then I have before.

Boldness

To me, boldness means to put yourself out there and express. To be bold with your ideas, which may seem outlandish at times and see them come to fruition is something that I find empowering and pushes me to take risks and explore.
